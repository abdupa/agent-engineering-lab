# Release records

## Archived — v0.1 to v0.5

These are the closure records of the **AI Engineering Laboratory**, the predecessor of
this repository. They are kept verbatim because they are evidence, and evidence is not
edited after the fact.

Consequence: a few internal links in them point at documents that lived in that
repository and do not exist here — `AGENTS.md` (superseded by [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)),
`BLUEPRINT.md` (superseded by [CHARTER.md](../CHARTER.md) and [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)),
and `study-guide/STUDY_GUIDE.md` (kept as [reference](../reference/v0.5-study-guide.md)).

Those links are left broken deliberately. Repointing them would change a record of what
was true at the time it was written.
